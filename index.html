About

  • I am a PhD candidate in Mathematics at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, expecting to complete my PhD in March 2027.
  • My PhD advisor is Prof. Hongze Li, Professor in the School of Mathematical Sciences at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, whose research focuses on analytic number theory.
  • I am currently a visiting research student at UNSW Sydney, working under the supervision of Prof. Igor E. Shparlinski.
  • My research is in analytic number theory, with particular interests in exponential and character sums, sieve methods, equidistribution, and Diophantine equations.

Research Interests

Areas of interest

  • Analytic number theory
  • Exponential sums, character sums, and bilinear forms
  • Kloosterman and Salié sums
  • Sieve methods, large sieve inequalities, and the distribution of primes
  • Equidistribution modulo one and discrepancy theory
  • Diophantine equations and the Hardy–Littlewood circle method

Current directions

  • Sign changes of Kloosterman sums over square-free almost-prime moduli
  • Moment estimates and discrepancy for sums of square roots modulo one
  • Large sieve inequalities for quadratic character sums over short intervals
  • Shifted bilinear sums of Salié sums and modular square roots of shifted primes
  • Least non-trivial zeros of cubic forms and systems of additive cubic equations
